1. Introduction

MSB=most significant bit

0 msb===> convert to decimal
1 msb===> take 2's compliment then add -ve sign
       @ 2's compliment is 1's compliment(toggle all bits) and binary 1(x0001) to it.
	

if there are n bits in a data type of number, then range of number would be -2^(n-1) to 2^(n-1)-1

Lets just say there is a data type DATA_TYPE with maximum 4 bits.

numbers would be.

0000	0
0001	1
0010	2
0011	3
0100	4
0101	5
0110	6
0111	7
1000   -8  two's compliment of (1000)''= 1's compliment(1000)' + 0001= 0111 + 0001= 1000= 8 with negative sign(-8)
1001   -7
1010   -6
1011   -5
1100   -4
1101   -3
1110   -2
1111   -1


Q.what if you store number out of range?
Ans.   lets just say DATA_TYPE a=12;
how a will be stored in memory= 1100 which is -4.
so when you print a it will print -4 not 12.
